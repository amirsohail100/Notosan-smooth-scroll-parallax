Journey to new frontiers — Noto Nature Park

VISIT

Far from the frenetic energy of Japan's great cities lies the ancient hamlet of Noto. Soaring peaks, tranquil lakes and endless waterfalls come together to form a habitat for an extraordinary range of species.

01 — Experience

Tranquility

Surprising and captivating in equal measure, Noto is unlike anywhere else. Its peaks, lakes and waterfalls shape a landscape of lasting calm, where the pace of life slows and every moment feels unhurried.

Learn more

02 — Habitat

Biodiversity

Noto's varied terrain supports remarkable wildlife, from rare birds and deer to delicate alpine flora. Together they form a thriving ecosystem that rewards patient and respectful exploration.
